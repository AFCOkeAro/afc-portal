## The message to send (copy-paste)

**To:** info@ndpc.gov.ng
**Subject:** Compliance Annual Return — scope question, faith-based organisation

Dear Sir/Madam,

We write to ask two questions about the Compliance Annual Return (CAR) under the Nigeria Data Protection Act General Application and Implementation Directive (GAID) 2025.

We are a faith-based organisation in Nigeria. Our registration status is governed by the Registration Guidance Notice, which exempts faith-based organisations from registration under paragraph 6(b).

**Question 1 — scope.** GAID Article 10(14) requires data controllers "within the categories of UHL and EHL" to file a CAR through a licensed DPCO. Article 7(c) sets the deadline as 31 March each year for those categories. Does the faith-based exemption in the Registration Guidance Notice remove only the section 44 registration duty, or does it also remove Ultra-High-Level / Extra-High-Level status for the purposes of Article 10? In short: **is a faith-based organisation within CAR scope at all, if it would otherwise fall in the UHL or EHL volume bands?**

**Question 2 — our filing history.** Our organisation was established before 12 June 2023, so Article 10(7) applies and the CAR is due "not later than 31st of March each year." We believe a return was filed in a previous cycle but we hold no receipt or Compliance Audit Returns Certificate. **What does the Commission treat as our filing history, and what is the correct route to file or cure a return for the 31 March 2026 cycle, if one is outstanding?**

For context: our expected data-subject count in any rolling six-month window is below 5,000, so we believe the applicable band, if any, would be Extra-High Level rather than Ultra-High.

Thank you for any guidance you can provide.

[Name]
[Role]
[Church name]
[Phone / email]
